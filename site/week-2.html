Week 2

Adapting a Coding Agent

Track

A generic agent is useful. An agent that knows your team's PR checklist, can query your database, and applies your coding standards automatically is transformative. This week you learn skills (how to encode knowledge), MCP (how to connect tools), and the deeper principles of context engineering.

Time budget

Pre-work: 189 min204 min core.

By the end of this week you can

0 of 4 ticked

Tick one once you can do it, after the pre-work or the live session.

  1. Write a SKILL.md that triggers on the right tasks, and say when a skill beats a rule in CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md.

  2. Connect an MCP server and explain what it adds to the agent's context and what it costs.

  3. Decide when to hand work to a subagent, and how to check what it returns.

  4. Apply two context-engineering strategies, such as compaction and just-in-time retrieval, to a long task.
