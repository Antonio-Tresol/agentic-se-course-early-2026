Cohort calendar

Your course owner shares a calendar.json for your cohort; an example lives at calendar.example.json. Load it once and the session dates appear on the overview and on each week. Without it the pages show no dates, and "You are here" follows your own progress. The calendar is saved in this browser only and is not part of your progress file, except that the progress file names the cohort.

Export

Send this file to the course owner at the end of the course. It holds the items you ticked and the minutes you logged, every self-check try with your answers, confidence and scores, the outcomes you ticked and your Week 1 estimates. It holds no name or email unless you type one as your alias.

Shown in the file name and to the course owner. Leave it empty to stay pseudonymous: the file then carries only a random id.

If downloading and copying are both blocked, select all, copy, paste it into a text file and save that as a .json file.

Import

Move your progress from another browser or device, or restore a copy you exported. Choose the file or paste its text; this page says what it holds before anything changes.
