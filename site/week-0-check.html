  1. Question 1 of 5 · choice

    Under the 4D framework for AI fluency, which competency focuses on critically assessing the quality, accuracy, and relevance of an AI model's output?

    Discernment is the ability to critically evaluate AI outputs for accuracy, bias, and quality. Delegation decides what tasks to allocate, Description articulates requirements, and Diligence ensures ethical, safe, and rigorous follow-through.

    Source: AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations, "The 4D Framework" (opens in a new tab). Also Session 0, slide 4.

  2. Question 2 of 5 · choice

    Which mode of engaging with AI describes a setup where the system operates autonomously within defined boundaries to plan and take actions toward a goal?

    In the Agency mode, AI autonomously takes actions and makes decisions within defined boundaries to achieve objectives. Automation executes predetermined routine workflows, while Augmentation pairs the human and AI interactively to enhance capability.

    Source: AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations, "Modes of Engagement" (opens in a new tab). Also Session 0, slide 4.

  3. Question 3 of 5 · choice

    According to the AI Capabilities and Limitations foundations, how does a large language model primarily generate text?

    Large language models operate on next-token prediction, calculating statistical probability distributions over tokens based on patterns learned during training and the current context. They do not query internal databases or run symbol compilers to produce prose.

    Source: AI Capabilities and Limitations, "Next Token Prediction" (opens in a new tab).

    An LLM's working memory corresponds to its context window, which contains only the tokens present in the current prompt and conversation history. Because the model weights remain fixed after training, no memory persists across separate sessions unless explicitly loaded via files, system prompts, or retrieval.

    Source: AI Capabilities and Limitations, "Working Memory" (opens in a new tab).
